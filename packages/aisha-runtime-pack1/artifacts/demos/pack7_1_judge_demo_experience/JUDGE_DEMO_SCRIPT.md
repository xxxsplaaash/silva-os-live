# Judge Demo Script

**Narrator Opening**:
"Normal AI assistants accumulate facts until they hallucinate. A.I.S.H.A doesn't just remember what you said. She preserves what changed, why it changed, and what consequence follows. Welcome to Article 9."

**First User/Player Command**:
*Player tells Mertens they were at the diplomatic dinner.*

**Contradiction Reveal**:
*Player later tells Mei they were in the vault corridor.*
"You've established two conflicting truths. Two NPCs can both be telling the truth, and that is the problem."

**Beckett Audit Pressure**:
"Beckett is approaching. If Mertens and Mei compare notes, your cover is blown. Burning a cover solves one problem while creating another."

**A.I.S.H.A Recommendation**:
*A.I.S.H.A flags the collision and recommends diverting Mei before she reaches Mertens.*
"The next move changes because the memory graph changed."

**Proof Trace Reveal**:
*The UI shows the dinner alibi marked as 'superseded' and the corridor alibi marked as 'active'.*
"The system remembers the lie as evidence, not as current truth. The proof trace shows why A.I.S.H.A said what she said."

**Closing Line**:
"A.I.S.H.A preserves history, updates active truth, and tracks consequence. This is contradiction-aware continuity."
